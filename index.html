Add FreshCheck to your Home Screen

Open FreshCheck quickly whenever you need to check your fridge.

On Android (Chrome)
  1. Open the hosted FreshCheck website in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮).
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app, if shown.
  4. Confirm the name and tap Add or Install.

Note: this downloadable preview needs to be hosted with a web app manifest and service worker for a full installable, offline-capable PWA experience.

About FreshCheck

FreshCheck helps households track food, remember dates, and make more mindful use of groceries.

Reminder: date labels and storage instructions vary by food. Never rely on the app alone to determine whether food is safe.
